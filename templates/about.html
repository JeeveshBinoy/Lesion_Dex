About LesionDex

Our Mission

LesionDex is dedicated to making skin cancer detection more accessible and efficient through cutting-edge artificial intelligence. Our goal is to assist dermatologists and healthcare professionals in early detection of potentially malignant skin lesions, ultimately improving patient outcomes.

The Technology

Our models is trained on a diverse dataset of dermatoscopic images, allowing it to recognize and classify seven different types of skin lesions:

  • Actinic Keratosis & Intraepithelial Carcinoma (AKIEC)
  • Basal Cell Carcinoma (BCC)
  • Benign Keratosis (BKL)
  • Dermatofibroma (DF)
  • Melanoma (MEL)
  • Melanocytic Nevus (NV)
  • Vascular Lesion (VASC)

The model analyzes visual patterns, colors, shapes, and textures that might be difficult for the human eye to discern, providing additional insight to support clinical decision-making.

Important Disclaimer

LesionDex is designed as a supportive tool for healthcare professionals and should not replace proper medical diagnosis. The predictions should always be verified by qualified medical practitioners. If you have concerns about a skin lesion, please consult with a dermatologist or healthcare provider immediately.

Our Team

LesionDex was developed by a multidisciplinary team of dermatologists, AI researchers, and software engineers committed to advancing healthcare technology. We continuously work to improve our algorithms and expand our capabilities.
